# Northwind SQL Business Analysis

## Dataset Overview
The **Northwind SQLite Database** is a sample business database that contains information about customers, orders, products, categories, suppliers, and employees. This project uses SQL and Pandas to analyze sales performance and customer behavior.

---

## Business Questions

- Find the Top 10 Selling Products
- Identify the Top 10 Customers by Revenue
- Analyze Monthly Sales Trends
- Discover the Best-Performing Product Categories
- Calculate Customer Purchase Frequency

---

## Data Analysis

- Imported SQL query results into Pandas.
- Performed a brief exploratory analysis.
- Checked data types, summary statistics, and missing values.
- Documented key business insights.

---

## Key Business Insights

1. **Louisiana Hot Spiced Okra** is the top-selling product with **206,213 units sold**.
2. **B's Beverages** generated the highest revenue of **6.15 million**.
3. Monthly sales remained relatively stable from **2012 to 2023**, with moderate fluctuations.
4. The **Beverages** category generated the highest revenue (**92.16 million**), making it the best-performing category.
5. **B's Beverages** placed the highest number of orders (**210**), highlighting the importance of repeat customers.
